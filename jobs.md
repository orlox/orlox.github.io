## Postdoctoral Funding opportunities

There are various fellowship schemes that can be applied to to join my group at Ghent University. If you think you could be a good fit within my research group, feel free to contact me about these possibilities. The Flemish funding agency (FWO) provides two postdoctoral fellowship schemes for varying levels of seniority:

- [FWO Junior Postdoctoral Fellowship](https://www.fwo.be/en/support-programmes/all-calls/postdoctoral-researchers/junior-postdoctoral-fellowship/): Normally due mid-November.
- [FWO Senior Postdoctoral Fellowship](https://www.fwo.be/en/support-programmes/all-calls/postdoctoral-researchers/senior-postdoctoral-fellowship/): Normally due mid-November.

The European Union also has the Marie Curie Postdoctoral fellowships which can be brought to European institutions. For candidates who already have a PhD and have an excellent track record this is a great opportunity:

- [MSCA Postdoctoral Fellowships](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/calls/msca-postdoctoral-fellowships-2025)

## PhD Funding opportunities

The FWO also has fellowship scheme for PhD students:

- [FWO PhD Fellowship fundamental research](https://www.fwo.be/en/support-programmes/all-calls/phd/phd-fellowship-fundamental-research/): Normally due beginning of March.

Additional funding possibilities could be present through Ghent University. In particular, for students of developing countries in the global south, there is the possibility to [co-fund a doctoral degree](https://www.ugent.be/en/research/funding/bof/dos). This requires joint supervision from a promoter at Ghent University and another one in the partner University located at the other country, with both parties contributing financially.
